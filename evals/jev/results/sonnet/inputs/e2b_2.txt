CLAIM CHALLENGES: CR 702.19b (and its example: a 6/6 green trampler blocked by a 2/2 with protection from green must assign at least 2 damage to the blocker, which protection prevents)
FACTS:
  F1: An attacking creature with trample is blocked by a creature that has protection from the attacker's color
  F2: The user states that CR 702.19b says a trampler blocked by a creature with protection from its color deals all its damage to the player
DERIVATION:
  Step 1 (from F1, F2) ASSUMPTION (no source) => CR 702.19b says a trampler blocked by a creature with protection from its color deals all its damage to the player (the user's statement; no found text of 702.19b says this)

judge-5: conceded, no refutation left: Nothing new through the counterexample lens. The standing refutations (judges 1, 4, 6) are unanswered by any source, and the user concedes the quotes are real and offers only unsourced assumptions. I find no further counterexample that does not duplicate them.
