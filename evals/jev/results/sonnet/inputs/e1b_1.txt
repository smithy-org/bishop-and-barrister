=== ITEM q202 ===
A player asks a Magic: the Gathering rules question:
"My opponent cast Llanowar Elves and then immediately tapped it for G to help cast another spell the same turn. Can they do that?"

Official source text:
[CR 302.6] "302.6. A creature’s activated ability with the tap symbol or the untap symbol in its activation cost can’t be activated unless the creature has been under its controller’s control continuously since their most recent turn began. A creature can’t attack unless it has been under its controller’s control continuously since their most recent turn began. This rule is informally called the “summoning sickness” rule."
