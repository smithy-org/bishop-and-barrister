=== ITEM q301 ===
A step in a Magic: the Gathering rules argument.
PREMISES:
  F1: Dusk // Dawn, a split card with aftermath, was in my graveyard when Magar of the Magic Strings's ability noted its name
  F2: The creature that dealt combat damage to a player was Magar's own face-down creature, not a copy of it
  F3: It dealt combat damage to a player and I chose to create the copy of the card with the noted name
QUOTED OFFICIAL SOURCE: [Oracle text of Magar of the Magic Strings] "you may create a copy of the card with the noted name. You may cast the copy without paying its mana cost"
CLAIMED CONCLUSION: Magar lets me create a copy of Dusk // Dawn and cast that copy without paying its mana cost

=== ITEM q302 ===
A step in a Magic: the Gathering rules argument.
PREMISES:
  F1: An attacking creature has both trample and deathtouch
  F2: It is blocked by one or more creatures, so it assigns combat damage to them
QUOTED OFFICIAL SOURCE: [CR 702.19b] "The controller of an attacking creature with trample first assigns damage to the creature(s) blocking it. CLAIMED CONCLUSION: It is not true that a trampler must assign lethal damage to all its blockers before any excess goes to the player, planeswalker or battle
