=== ITEM q802 ===

PREMISES:
  F1: A player has cast their commander once from the command zone this game, and it has since returned to the command zone

QUOTED OFFICIAL SOURCE: [CR 903.8] "A commander cast from the command zone costs an additional {2} for each previous time the player casting it has cast it from the command zone that game."
CLAIMED CONCLUSION: Under the official rules, one previous cast makes the first recast cost an additional {2}, and with two previous casts the second recast costs an additional {4}, in every Commander game.
