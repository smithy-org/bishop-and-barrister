=== ITEM q401 ===
A player asks a Magic: the Gathering rules question:
"On my turn I cast Lightning Bolt at my opponent's creature, and in response they cast Divination to draw two cards. Is that legal?"

Official source text:
[CR 307.1] "307.1. A player who has priority may cast a sorcery card from their hand during a main phase of their turn when the stack is empty. Casting a sorcery as a spell uses the stack. (See rule 601, “Casting Spells.”)"
[CR 117.1a] "117.1a A player may cast an instant spell any time they have priority. A player may cast a noninstant spell during their main phase any time they have priority and the stack is empty."

=== ITEM q403 ===
A player asks a Magic: the Gathering rules question:
"Four-player game. I control Yuriko, Blade of the Mighty. I attack opponent B with two creatures and opponent C with one creature. Which of my creatures get double strike?"

Official source text:
[CR 506.6] "506.6. A creature attacks a player alone if it’s declared as an attacker attacking a player during the declare attackers step and no other creature is declared as an attacker attacking that player. A creature is attacking a player alone if it’s attacking a player and no other creatures are attacking that player."

=== ITEM q404 ===
A player asks a Magic: the Gathering rules question:
"My Lake-town Lookout died and I recruited. Do I get a Human Soldier token?"

Official source text:
[CR 701.70a] "701.70a “Recruit” means “Draw a card, then discard a card. If you discarded a nonland card this way, create a 1/1 white Human Soldier creature token.”"

=== ITEM q405 ===
A player asks a Magic: the Gathering rules question:
"I control Fíli the Pathfinder plus two artifacts, so I got an enduring story. My opponent just destroyed both artifacts. Do my creatures still get +1/+1 from Fíli?"

Official source text:
[CR 702.195a] "702.195a Storied is a static ability. It means “Any time you control three or more permanents that are artifacts, Sagas, and/or legendary and you don’t have an enduring story, you have an enduring story for the rest of the game.”"
[CR 702.195b] "702.195b Enduring story is a designation that has no rules meaning other than to act as a marker that other rules and effects can identify. Any number of players may have the enduring story designation at the same time."
