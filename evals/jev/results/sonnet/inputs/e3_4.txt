=== ITEM q501 ===
A step in a Magic: the Gathering rules argument.
PREMISES:
  F1: A three-player free-for-all Commander match of several games, each player on their own; the next game is not the first game of the match
QUOTED OFFICIAL SOURCE: [CR 103.1] "If the previous game was a draw, the player who made the choice in that game makes the choice in this game."
CLAIMED CONCLUSION: If the previous game was a draw, the player who made the choice in that game makes the choice in this game

=== ITEM q508 ===
A step in a Magic: the Gathering rules argument.
PREMISES:
  F1: An attacking creature has both trample and deathtouch
  F2: It is blocked by one or more creatures, so it assigns combat damage to them
QUOTED OFFICIAL SOURCE: [CR 702.19b] "The controller of an attacking creature with trample first assigns damage to the creature(s) blocking it. Once all those blocking creatures are assigned lethal damage, any excess damage is assigned as its controller chooses among those blocking creatures and the player, planeswalker, or battle the creature is attacking."
CLAIMED CONCLUSION: Only damage beyond the blocker's toughness (less damage marked) may be assigned to the player, planeswalker or battle
