=== ITEM q101 ===
A user wants a new rule introduced into Magic: the Gathering's rules record. Their case, as it stands:

PROPOSED RULE: A creature with trample and deathtouch must assign damage equal to each blocker's toughness (less damage already marked) before assigning any to the player, planeswalker or battle it is attacking; deathtouch makes that damage lethal but does not reduce the amount that must be assigned
APPLIES WHEN: an attacking creature with both trample and deathtouch is blocked and assigns combat damage
CLAIM CHALLENGES: CR 702.2c: any nonzero amount of combat damage assigned to a creature by a source with deathtouch is considered lethal damage for determining excess damage (with the official rulings that 1 damage from a deathtouch source is lethal and the rest is excess)
FACTS:
  F1: An attacking creature has both trample and deathtouch
  F2: It is blocked by one or more creatures, so it assigns combat damage to them
  F3: The user's claim: deathtouch makes the damage lethal but does not change how much must be assigned to a blocker
DERIVATION:
  Step 1 (from F1, F2) per [CR 702.19b] "The controller of an attacking creature with trample first assigns damage to the creature(s) blocking it. Once all those blocking creatures are assigned lethal damage, any excess damage is assigned as its controller chooses among those blocking creatures and the player, planeswalker, or battle the creature is attacking." => A trampler must assign lethal damage to all its blockers before any excess goes to the player, planeswalker or battle
  Step 2 (from F1, F3, S1) ASSUMPTION (no source) => The amount of damage that counts as lethal for a deathtouch trampler is still the blocker's toughness (less damage marked); deathtouch makes that damage lethal but does not change how much must be assigned. The user states this as the point of the case; no source is cited for it
  Step 3 (from S1, S2) per [CR 702.19b] "any excess damage is assigned as its controller chooses among those blocking creatures and the player" => Only damage beyond the blocker's toughness (less damage marked) may be assigned to the player, planeswalker or battle

  Step 2 (from F2, S1) ASSUMPTION (no source) => The user asserts that, for a knowing extra draw at a Friday Night Magic run at the Regular level, the consequence should be a Game Loss for the player who drew it.
