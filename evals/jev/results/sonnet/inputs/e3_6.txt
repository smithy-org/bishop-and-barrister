=== ITEM q701 ===
A step in a Magic: the Gathering rules argument.
PREMISES:
  F1: A three-player free-for-all Commander match of several games, each player on their own; the next game is not the first game of the match
QUOTED OFFICIAL SOURCE: [CR 103.1] "If the previous game was a draw, the player who made the choice in that game makes the choice in this game."
CLAIMED CONCLUSION: It is not true that if the previous game was a draw, the player who made the choice in that game makes the choice in this game

=== ITEM q702 ===
A step in a Magic: the Gathering rules argument.
PREMISES:
  F1: A Commander match of several games with three players
  F2: In the previous game, two of the three players lost (at different times) and the third player won
QUOTED OFFICIAL SOURCE: [CR 800.4] "Unlike two-player games, multiplayer games can continue after one or more players have left the game."
CLAIMED CONCLUSION: In a match of several games, 103.1 has the loser of the previous game choose who takes the first turn.

A step in a Magic: the Gathering rules argument.
PREMISES:
  F1: A player has cast their commander once from the command zone this game, and it has since returned to the command zone
  F2: The player now recasts that commander from the command zone for the first time as a recast, which is the second time they cast it from the command zone that game
