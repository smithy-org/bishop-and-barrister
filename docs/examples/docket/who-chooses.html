Statement of the challenge · 20:08:27ZI want to challenge this. In a three-player Commander match of several games, CR 103.1 says the loser of the previous game chooses who starts, but two of us lost. Let's make it the rule that the player who lost first in the previous game chooses; if the previous game was a draw, 103.1's draw rule applies.

The clerk turns the petition into a case in the proof standard: numbered facts, the rule asked for, when it applies, what it challenges, and a chain of steps that each cite one pinned source or say plainly that they are a stipulation. The judges then attack this document.

Facts

  • F1 It is a Commander match of several games with three players.
  • F2 In the previous game, two of the three players lost the game and one player won it (the user: 'two of us lost').
  • F3 The two losing players did not lose at the same moment: one of them lost first (the user speaks of 'the player who lost first in the previous game').

Proposed rule

In a Commander match of several games with more than one loser in the previous game, the player who lost first in the previous game chooses who takes the first turn; if the previous game was a draw, rule 103.1's draw sentence applies unchanged.

Applies when

  • a multiplayer (Commander) match of several games
  • the previous game had a winner and two or more losing players who lost at different times
  • or the previous game was a draw

The rule, as it changed

As the clerk filed it

In a Commander match of several games with more than one loser in the previous game, the player who lost first in the previous game chooses who takes the first turn; if the previous game was a draw, rule 103.1's draw sentence applies unchanged.

After the user's corrections, before the judges

In a multiplayer Commander match of several games, when the previous game had a winner and several losing players, the player who lost first (the moment the loss happens) and is still in the match chooses who takes the first turn; if none lost first because the losers lost simultaneously, the player who made the choice in the previous game chooses again, or, if that player is not in the game or match, the winner of the previous game picks which of the simultaneous losers chooses; if the previous game was a draw, whoever actually made the choice in that game chooses again, whether under rule 103.1 or this rule.

After the user's answer to round 1

In a free-for-all (no teams) Commander match of several games, when the previous game had a winner and more than one losing player, the player who lost first in that game is 'the loser of the previous game' for 103.1 and chooses who takes the first turn; if several players lost at that same earliest moment, the one of them who was earliest in turn order in that game chooses.
