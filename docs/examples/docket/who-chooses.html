Statement of the challenge · 17:01:39ZI want to challenge this. In a three-player Commander match of several games, CR 103.1 says the loser of the previous game chooses who starts, but two of us lost. Let's make it the rule that the player who lost first in the previous game chooses; if the previous game was a draw, 103.1's draw rule applies.

Part 2 The clerk’s brief

The clerk turns the petition into a case in the proof standard: numbered facts, the rule asked for, when it applies, what it challenges, and a chain of steps that each cite one pinned source or say plainly that they are a stipulation. The judges then attack this document.

Facts

  • F1 A Commander match of several games with three players
  • F2 In the previous game, two of the three players lost (at different times) and the third player won

Proposed rule

In a multiplayer match of several games, if more than one player lost the previous game, the player who lost first in that game is the loser who chooses who takes the first turn. If the previous game was a draw, the draw rule of 103.1 applies unchanged.

Applies when

  • a multiplayer match of several games (such as a three-player Commander match) where the previous game was won by one player and more than one player lost it, or where the previous game was a draw

What it challenges

none: a gap (No official answer exists, so this fills a gap. A gap rule must say so, and its gap-filling step is flagged a stipulation.)

    In a match of several games, the loser of the previous game chooses who takes the first turn.CR 103.1
  2. Step 2 from F1, F2

    Unlike two-player games, multiplayer games can continue after one or more players have left the game.CR 800.4
  3. Step 3 stipulation from S1, S2

Refutation 1Overriddenagainst step 3

Step 3 stipulates that the first loser chooses. But in the case's own scenario (F2) every loser has lost, and a player who lost has left the game (CR 800.4 premise of the case itself: multiplayer games continue after players leave). 103.1 is a rule that requires 'the loser of the previous game' to make a choice. So the Comprehensive Rules already assign this choice by turn order, and the stipulation (first loser chooses, with a tie-break by agreement or random) contradicts 800.4h and cannot be accepted as a gap-filler. At minimum the case never addresses 800.4h, which is the specific rule on choices owed by players who left.

The rule, as it changed

As the clerk filed it

In a multiplayer match of several games, if more than one player lost the previous game, the player who lost first in that game is the loser who chooses who takes the first turn. If the previous game was a draw, the draw rule of 103.1 applies unchanged.

After the user's answer to round 1

In a free-for-all multiplayer match of several games (not a team game and not an Archenemy game), if the previous game was won by one player and more than one player lost it, the player who lost first in that game is the loser who chooses who takes the first turn. If several players lost first at the same moment, those players decide among themselves which of them chooses, and if they cannot agree the chooser is picked at random from them. If the previous game was a draw, the draw sentence of 103.1 applies unchanged and this rule does not apply. Team games and Archenemy games are not affected (103.1b stays as it is).

After the user's answer to round 2

In a free-for-all multiplayer match of several games, if the previous game was won by one player, every other player counts as a loser of that game for this rule: a player who left the game earlier lost at the moment they left, and a player still in the game when the winner won lost at the moment the game ended. If there is more than one loser, the player who lost first is the loser who chooses who takes the first turn. If several players lost first at the same moment, those players decide among themselves which of them chooses, and if they cannot agree the chooser is picked at random from them. If the previous game was a draw, the draw sentence of 103.1 applies unchanged and this rule does not apply. The rule does not apply to team games, or to any game type whose starting player the Comprehensive Rules already settle by their own rule, such as Archenemy games (103.1b) and Supervillain Rumble (904.12c); those stay as they are.

After the user's answer to round 3

In a free-for-all multiplayer match of several games, if the previous game was won by one player and no player left it because the game was a draw for them, the losers of that game are the players who left it by losing, each at the moment they lost, and any players still in the game when the winner won, at the moment the game ended. If there is more than one loser, the player who lost first is the loser who chooses who takes the first turn. If several players lost first at the same moment, those players decide among themselves which of them chooses, and if they cannot agree the chooser is picked at random from them. If the previous game was a draw, or was a draw for any player, the draw sentence of 103.1 applies and this rule does not apply. The rule does not apply to team games, or to any game type whose starting player the Comprehensive Rules already settle by their own rule, such as Archenemy games (103.1b) and Supervillain Rumble (904.12c); those stay as they are.

The rule as recorded

Introduced at level 5

In a free-for-all multiplayer match of several games, if the previous game was won by one player and no player left it because the game was a draw for them, the losers of that game are the players who left it by losing, each at the moment they lost, and any players still in the game when the winner won, at the moment the game ended. Stipulation, carried with the rule

In a free-for-all game with one winner in which no player left because the game was a draw for them, the losers for 103.1 are the players who left by losing and the players still in the game when the winner won. Where there is more than one loser, the player who lost first chooses who takes the first turn; players who lost first at the same moment decide among themselves who chooses, else it is random among them. Draws (whole game or for any player), team games and game types whose starting player the CR already settles (Archenemy, Supervillain Rumble) are outside this stipulation.
