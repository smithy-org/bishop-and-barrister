# Pauper

| Rule          | Value                                                                                  | Source (level)                    |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Card pool     | Commons only: a card is legal in any printing if it was ever released at common in a paper product or on Magic Online, including lands printed with both a common set symbol and the L rarity code. A common promo is legal only if the card meets that test | Wizards' Pauper page (1)          |
| Deck          | At least 60 cards                                                                      | CR 100.2a (1)                     |
| Copies        | At most four of a card, deck and sideboard together, basic lands excepted              | CR 100.2a, 100.4a (1); Pauper page (1) |
| Sideboard     | Up to 15 cards, if used                                                                | CR 100.4a (1); Pauper page (1)    |
| Starting life | 20                                                                                     | CR 103.4 (1)                      |

- Pauper isn't one of the MTR's Constructed formats (section 6 covers Standard, Modern, Vintage, Legacy and
  Pioneer). Its banned list is on the Pauper page and in `card.py`'s legality.
- Matches, sideboarding and starting a game: `matches.md`.
