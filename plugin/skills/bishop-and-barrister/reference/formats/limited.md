# Limited: Draft, Sealed

| Rule          | Value                                                                                    | Source (level)                    |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Deck          | At least 40 cards, no maximum                                                            | CR 100.2b (1); MTR 7.1 (2)        |
| Copies        | No four-card limit: as many as the product held                                          | CR 100.2b (1); MTR 7.1 (2)        |
| Sideboard     | Every card in the pool that isn't in the deck                                            | CR 100.4b (1); MTR 7.1 (2)        |
| Basic lands   | Any number of Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain and Forest may be added to the deck and sideboard. Not Snow-Covered basics or Wastes, even where they're legal | MTR 7.2 (2) |
| Card pool     | Only cards opened or drafted by that player, from the boosters used; MTR 7.2 lists the few exceptions | MTR 7.2 (2)          |
| Starting life | 20                                                                                       | CR 103.4 (1)                      |

- **Changing the deck between matches (continuous construction):** at tournaments without decklists, players
  may swap cards between deck and sideboard between matches and keep the changes. The Head Judge or organizer
  must announce before deck construction if this isn't allowed, and it's never allowed at Competitive or
  Professional REL (MTR 7.3). Otherwise the deck and sideboard return to their registered form before each
  match (MTR 3.16).
- **Product:** each player gets the same product, new and unopened; six play boosters are recommended for
  Sealed and three for Draft (MTR 7.2). Draft procedure is MTR 7.7.
- Matches, sideboarding and starting a game: `matches.md`.
